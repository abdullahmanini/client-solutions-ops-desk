# Metric guide for the sales team (Q3 2026, client accounts)

| Metric | Formula | Q3 | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPM | Spend / impressions x 1,000 | $6.90 | Treating a cheaper CPM as better quality; search placements cost more per view and convert better |
| CTR | Clicks / impressions | 1.07% | Calling a higher CTR better results; a click is not a purchase |
| CVR | Conversions / clicks | 2.99% | Dividing by impressions, which mixes CTR into it |
| CPA | Spend / conversions | $21.57 | Comparing products across different account mixes |
| ROAS | Conversion value / spend | 2.51x | Averaging account ROAS instead of total value / total spend |
| Adoption | Accounts with $500+ on a product in 4 weeks / active accounts | Auto-Campaigns 28.9% | Counting internal test accounts |
| Attainment | Delivered / committed spend | 101% | Netting overs against unders hides the 36 accounts under 90% |
